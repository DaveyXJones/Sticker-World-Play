Sticker Worlds

A sticker book where cabinets, fridges, windows and planets open into whole new scenes.

  • Turn the pages. Swipe, or tap the folded corner. Tap the book up top to see every page or add one.
  • Pinch to zoom into the cabinet, the fridge or a galaxy. Double-tap one to step right in.
  • Add stickers and drag them onto shelves, or into a cupboard to put them away.
  • Tap things. Doors open, lamps light up, the cat purrs. And Pip the mouse hides somewhere new every day.
